## Design Context

### Users
Researchers, graduate students and professionals reading English and Korean papers and books in a local macOS study workspace. They follow source evidence, explore concepts and revisit saved learning materials. Visible study prose defaults to Korean.

### Brand Personality
Calm, thoughtful, precise. The interface should support sustained reading and make learning progress clear without competing with the PDF.

### Aesthetic Direction
shadcn/ui-inspired: concise and refined. A neutral zinc scale with white surfaces, a near-black primary, 1px borders, 6–12px radii and soft layered shadows; light and dark follow the system appearance. Tabs are segmented controls (muted track, raised active pill) or underlined lines; statuses are small outline badges with a colored dot. Sans-serif UI type throughout, with serif kept only for quoted source passages. Color is reserved for meaning: green for independently passed checks and document-supported claims, blue for inference, amber for missing evidence, red for destructive actions. Section and goal lists keep their compact numbered titles, progressive disclosure and quiet hover actions.

### Design Principles
- Let titles and questions carry the reading flow; reveal supporting detail on demand.
- Keep source evidence accessible and preserve exploration versus independently passed checks.
- Group primary reading/study actions separately from editing and record management.
- Adapt to the sidebar width, long Korean questions, keyboard use and reduced motion.
- Preserve the reader, saved goals, study records and existing app behavior while refining presentation.
