## Design Context

### Users
Researchers, graduate students and professionals reading English and Korean papers and books in a local macOS study workspace. They follow source evidence, explore concepts and revisit saved learning materials. Visible study prose defaults to Korean.

### Brand Personality
Calm, thoughtful, precise. The interface should support sustained reading and make learning progress clear without competing with the PDF.

### Aesthetic Direction
Built on the Astryx design system with its neutral theme: restrained warm grays, white surfaces on a light gray canvas, 1px hairline borders, 6/10/12px radii and soft layered shadows; light and dark follow the system appearance through `light-dark()` tokens. Native macOS type (SF with Korean fallbacks); serif only for quoted source passages. Chrome stays quiet and gets out of the page's way: one title bar, a floating page dock on the canvas, floating cards for selected text and menus. Answers read as prose with inline page citations. Color is reserved for meaning: green for independently passed checks and document-supported claims, blue for inference and citations, amber for missing evidence, red for destructive actions. Use Astryx components (`bunx astryx component <Name>`) before hand-styling a control; see `docs/decisions/008-astryx-design-system.md` for the layer and token bridge.

### Design Principles
- Let titles and questions carry the reading flow; reveal supporting detail on demand.
- Keep source evidence accessible and preserve exploration versus independently passed checks.
- Group primary reading/study actions separately from editing and record management.
- Adapt to the sidebar width, long Korean questions, keyboard use and reduced motion.
- Preserve the reader, saved goals, study records and existing app behavior while refining presentation.
